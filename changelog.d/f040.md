- **factory 0.4.0** (`factory/`, lane f040; ADR-0016, spec
  `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-factory-040-design.md`; full entry in `factory/CHANGELOG.md`):
  the harness fails closed and read-only agents are enforced.
  - Guards fail closed: the wrappers block on any non-zero exit, the command splitter failing denies,
    and an in-hook watchdog kills a slow count or dispatch-check git read.
  - Ledger: every append goes through `ledger.pending.jsonl` and is folded under the dispatch lock
    (fold first, owner-token locks); `scripts/lane compact`; `FACTORY_LEDGER_LINES`.
  - Heredoc terminators are exact, and an ambiguous `WORD)` is refused.
  - Read-only agents (critic, reviewer, verifier) are enforced by `agent_type`: an allowlist by
    subcommand, fixed option sets, scratch-only writes, trusted checkouts, case-insensitive `.git`.
  - One report protocol: rendered fragments, `Verdict:`/`Head:` for read-only agents, `Status:` for
    write agents, report naming, and `lane audit --report` binding a report to a named dispatch.
  - `/lane-review` and `/ci-triage`; package-only commands leave the coordinator profile.
  - The cap is stated once; Precedence and guard-writing sections; an advisory format hook;
    `lane stats`/`lane handoff`; a new `verifier` agent.
  - Merged with 0.3.1 (owning-repo TDD ledger, unnamed TDD roles) and 0.3.2 (format fixtures at
    any depth, coordinator-profile format guard).
- The coordinator and the packages take 0.4.0 in their own sync lanes. Each needs the seeded edits
  listed under Migration in `factory/CHANGELOG.md`.

- **factory 0.3.2** (`factory/`, lane tw-035): a fix for the coordinator profile.
  - `ci.jl format` (`CI.format_files`) now skips a `scripts/test/fixtures/` directory at any
    depth, not only at the repo root. The coordinator vendors `factory/template/`, so 0.3.1
    selected the template's deliberately broken fixtures there (sync-031: 3 fails in
    `test_ci_format.jl`).
  - The factory's own Runic-clean test now covers only factory-managed scripts (the
    `.factory-version` `[managed]` files plus `factory/template/scripts/`), not the host's own
    `.jl` files.
  - The coordinator-profile guard (`test_profile_coordinator.jl`) vendors `factory/` and an
    unformatted `contracts/` file into its scratch coordinator, and runs `test_ci_format.jl`,
    `test_gen.jl` and `test_check_docs.jl` by default (plus `test_ci.jl` with
    `FACTORY_GUARD_FULL=1`).
  - Known limit: the coordinator's `contracts/` and `reference/prototype/` are not Runic-clean
    and the coordinator has no format CI job; nothing was reformatted.
- The coordinator and the packages on 0.3.1 take 0.3.2 in their own sync lanes.
